How to create waypoints

Waypoints are used to store referenced points that may be used for several purposes, one of them being the creation of routes. First select the "route and waypoints perspective" to have the view of the waypoints. Then:
  • select the popup menu "add"
  • or click on the green + button on top of the waypoint view
  • or last, drag a route point from the route view in the waypoint view

With the 2 first options, you will have to enter, in the dialog that will appear, the name and coordinates of the waypoint and to select a symbol. To see the waypoints on the chart, the button with a red flag, on top of the waypoint view must be selected.
You can then save the waypoints table under a new name (menu waypoints) and also import another waypoint table from another gpx file.
