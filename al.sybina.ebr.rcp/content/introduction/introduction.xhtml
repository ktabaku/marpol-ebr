Introduction

Capcode is a computer tool for sailors with a specialization in regatta

The main functions are:
1) Acquisition of NMEA messages from your instruments (GPS, speed, wind..., AIS)
2) Display of the boat location on a map (Raster or S-57 compatible)
3) Capture the polar of the boat speed and use for best speed, VMG, route, direct visualisation on the chart, etc.
4) Display different types of repeated (Wind, Speed, GPS)
5) Manage routes and waypoints with gpx support
6) Display chart and the world in 3D (with worldwind)
7) Display and detect collision routes with other vessel with A.I.S capabilities
... and other functions depending on which plugins are installed

what to do first

1) Configure the path for your charts

To do so:
a) Open the Window/preference and select the charts panel
b) in Path for charts, select New... and navigate to the directory on your computer where charts (BSB, S57) are stored. You can add several path by repeating this as many time as needed.
When you validate, capcode will build a catalog to reference your charts and to display a rectangle on the main chart view to show that a chart is available at this location. To make a chart visible, go to the "Navigation perspective" and right-click inside a chart rectangle then select "load chart".
